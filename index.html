Memory Garden: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Developer: Mr.Kut · Contact: mrkutapp@gmail.com

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, garden, achievements, statistics, settings and in-game items) is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. If Android backup is enabled, Android may include it in your device backup.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Google Play Games, and a copy of your game save is stored in your Google account ("Saved games") so you can restore it on another device. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels players start and finish) and Firebase Crashlytics to fix crashes. These services collect app interaction events, device information and an app instance identifier. They do not collect your name or contact details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional ads between levels. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and collect device information to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game sends at most two a day: a daily reminder, lives refilled, a streak about to end, a daily reward ready, and a come-back nudge. You can turn each type off in Settings → Notifications.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress clears your progress on the device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. To remove your cloud save, delete it in Google Play Games. For analytics data, contact us at mrkutapp@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
